## Locked comparison rules

Optimization equality has **zero numerical tolerance**: retain complete request
and response membership, IDs/order, public values, reported IEEE bits, private
frames/bins, failures, error strings, exit status and required artifacts. It is
insufficient to retain a mean, correlation, selected outliers or only successful
rows. The observation adapter measures the candidate's actual frozen source;
the independent equations remain separate from that adapter.

CLI comparisons retain the existing explicit metadata exclusions for named
timing/resource fields and portable-model creation time. Scientific values,
input populations, packed records, rankings, uncertainty endpoints, domain
labels, exclusions and errors are not excluded. Raw streams and failed
comparisons remain retained. A reference comparison affected only by model
creation timestamps requires the existing separate, field-specific resolution
receipt; a numeric, classification or coverage difference cannot use that route.

Independent-reference bounds are the sealed domain policies, not optimization
allowances. In particular, the
[model reference policy](../scientific_remediation/models/REFERENCE_RECHECK.md)
retains exact historical values where required, f64 equation bounds of `1e-12`
absolute plus `2e-12` relative, two f32 ULPs for coefficients, and eight f32
epsilons absolute/relative for fit metrics. The original Student-t checks retain
`2e-10` absolute plus `2e-12` relative; near-unit-alpha analytic checks retain zero
absolute plus `2e-12` relative. These apply to their declared fields and cases.
They are not a blanket tolerance for every model value.

Geometry and kinetics residual maxima below describe particular completed
baseline campaigns. They do not become thresholds for accepting a changed
candidate. Every original independent classification, per-ID residual record,
radius/center/grid stratum, error and missing reference result must remain
accounted for.

## Limits that optimization cannot erase

The [convention ledger](../scientific_remediation/CONVENTIONS_AND_LIMITS.md),
[geometry results](../scientific_remediation/geometry/RESULTS.md),
[thermodynamic results](../scientific_remediation/thermodynamics/FINAL_V4.md), and
[model results](../scientific_remediation/models/REFERENCE_RECHECK.md) remain the
authoritative scoped explanations. In particular:

- **Finite geometry:** B1 retains 360 angular directions and phase sensitivity;
  the corrected campaign retains a `0.0199442 Å` coordination-B1 rotation change.
  f32 lattice/occupancy boundaries, positive-zero region assignment and separately
  normalized odd-grid partitions remain. No universal Morfeus/Kraken equality or
  exact finite-grid partition/rotation identity is asserted.
- **Reference conventions:** native Bondi-style extensions/fallbacks differ for
  B, Br and other elements; unit-bond-vector centers differ from primary Kraken
  raw-vector centers. The baseline full matching-radius stratum's largest total
  mean residual is `0.00777188794 Å³`; the all-element maximum is `2.84793579 Å³`.
  Both remain in per-ID records and strata. The 56 matching-table structures
  with differing integer occupancy were reduced to 58 boundary decisions.
  These observed maxima are not permissive new tolerances.
- **Input information and degeneracy:** XYZ's 1.3-times-covalent-radius rule is
  an inference. Explicit topology/axes/centers carry additional information.
  Planar clearance intervals propagate coordinate rounding for a fixed computed
  normal; they do not enclose uncertainty in the normal or the full geometry.
  Very large translations can destroy f32 input detail; finite inputs need not
  imply representable outputs. The implemented Kraken property subset is not
  the entire published descriptor family.
- **Populations and kinetics:** uniform fallback is not inferred Boltzmann
  weighting; finite MMFF iteration-limit states do not imply convergence, and
  finite conformer search/electronic energies do not establish equilibrium free
  energies. Historical 298.15 K populations remain distinct from 353.15 K Ni-hDA
  response metadata. Nine original kinetic requests are invalid or outside the
  bounded Decimal comparison; checked/log APIs have separate extreme-input
  tests. The baseline normal-rate maximum relative residual `5.29218e-8` and ee
  maximum absolute residual `3.69286e-6` percentage points are scoped observations.
  A barrier difference does not determine an absolute rate; unsigned ee does
  not determine R/S, and exact 100% ee is a censored/infinite-limit condition.
- **Statistical implementation and interpretation:** nominal OLS includes its
  retained tiny ridge floor; VIF saturation, finite solver stopping and limited
  special-function range retain their declared scope. Fixed-feature LOO,
  nested-alpha and permutation diagnostics do not validate the complete feature
  search. Bootstrap generation/RNG is not independently reimplemented in the
  reference campaign; propagation uses retained replicates. Nominal Student-t
  intervals assume an adequate fixed linear model and residual distribution;
  marginal coefficient bands have no guaranteed simultaneous coverage.
  Descriptor support labels are not calibrated chemical reliability.
- **Unchecked API compatibility:** C2's cached evaluator is differential-tested
  against the unchanged public interval method, including the zero-parameter
  malformed-geometry compatibility edge. CLI model loading rejects that metadata.
  Preserving a direct API result is not independent validation of an invalid
  statistical interval.
- **Predictive/source evidence:** 92/141 historical nominal interval coverage,
  including 70/108 labeled interpolation, remains unfavorable and involves
  overlapping panels. Seven of ten historical outer folds failed. Historical
  ligand 723 is not scaffold-disjoint; reaction-transfer panels reuse ligands.
  Study 011's reported 0.158 top-1 recovery versus 0.333 random and 10/57 failed
  screens remain historical evidence. The Ni-hDA 2064 source conflict, missing
  original Kraken thermochemistry and some published-classifier/source-version
  discrepancies remain unresolved. Formula equality does not prove isomer
  identity. No prospective measurements were added.
